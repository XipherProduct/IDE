## OpenAI

**Models:** GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini, o1, o1-mini, o3-mini

OpenAI is the creator of ChatGPT. Their models excel at general-purpose tasks, coding, and creative writing.

## Anthropic

**Models:** Claude 4 Opus, Claude 4 Sonnet, Claude 3.5 Haiku

Anthropic's Claude models are known for their safety, long context windows, and nuanced understanding.

## Google

**Models:** Gemini 2.5 Pro, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini 2.0 Flash-Lite

Google's Gemini models offer strong multimodal capabilities and fast inference.

## DeepSeek

**Models:** DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1

DeepSeek provides powerful reasoning models at competitive prices. DeepSeek-R1 excels at math and coding.

## xAI

**Models:** Grok-3, Grok-3-mini

xAI's Grok models are trained with real-time information and provide unique perspectives.

## Zhipu AI

**Models:** GLM-4-Plus, GLM-4-Flash

Zhipu AI offers bilingual (Chinese/English) models with strong performance on Chinese language tasks.

## Moonshot

**Models:** Kimi k2

Moonshot's Kimi models specialize in long-context understanding, supporting up to 2M tokens.
